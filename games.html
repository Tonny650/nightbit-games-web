Legacy of the First Sun

In Development

A sci-fi survival adventure set on the micro-planet Erebus-9, where a collapsing containment dome threatens the last remnants of life. Explore unstable biomes, survive mutated ecosystems, and unravel the mystery behind the forbidden First Sun Protocol—an experiment that doomed the colony.

Key Features:

  • Open-world exploration with biome streaming
  • Progressive dome-decay survival system
  • Mutated wildlife with adaptive AI behaviors
  • Energy towers that regulate planetary stability
  • Lore-rich narrative tied to the First Sun Protocol
