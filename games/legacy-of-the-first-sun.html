LEGACY OF THE FIRST SUN

A first-person sci-fi survival horror set on Nyx-65, a forbidden biological experiment left to evolve on its own. As a stranded space scavenger, you must occupy abandoned infrastructures, manage scarce resources, and survive within a living ecosystem that adapts to every intrusion. The planet does not hunt you — it simply responds.

KEY FEATURES

Systemic survival focused on infrastructure occupation and maintenance

Living ecosystem with territorial, opportunistic and adaptive organisms

Scarce, improvised combat where every encounter carries risk

Environmental storytelling through abandoned facilities and biological remnants

Progression through practical learning and camp-based upgrades, not abstract XP
